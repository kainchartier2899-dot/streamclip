StreamClip Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 6, 2026

StreamClip is a desktop application designed to help authorized users create short-form clips from livestream content and publish those clips to social media accounts they connect to the application.

Information We Access

When a user connects a supported social media account, StreamClip may receive information made available through that platform's authorized API, such as an account identifier, display name, profile information, authorization tokens, and permissions granted by the user.

TikTok Data

StreamClip uses TikTok's official authorization and Content Posting interfaces. StreamClip only accesses a TikTok account after the account owner explicitly authorizes the application.

Depending on the permissions granted, StreamClip may access basic account information and receive authorization credentials needed to publish content to the connected account.

How Information Is Used

Information accessed by StreamClip is used to:

  • Authenticate connected accounts.
  • Provide the application's clipping and publishing functionality.
  • Publish user-authorized video content to connected accounts.
  • Maintain application functionality and prevent duplicate processing.

Authentication Credentials

StreamClip does not request or store a user's TikTok password. Authentication is performed through the applicable platform's authorization system.

Authorization tokens required for connected-account functionality are stored for the purpose of maintaining the authorized connection. Users may revoke platform access through the applicable platform's account settings.

Content

StreamClip processes video content that the user is authorized to use. Generated clips may be temporarily or permanently stored on the user's computer according to the user's application configuration.

Sharing of Information

StreamClip does not sell personal information. Information is shared with third-party platforms only as necessary to provide functionality requested and authorized by the user, such as publishing a video to a connected social media account.

Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored application information may be deleted by removing the applicable StreamClip application data from the user's computer. Users may disconnect StreamClip from a social media platform by revoking the application's authorization through that platform.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect authentication credentials and other application data. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when StreamClip's functionality or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.

Contact

Questions or requests concerning this Privacy Policy may be directed to the StreamClip application operator.
